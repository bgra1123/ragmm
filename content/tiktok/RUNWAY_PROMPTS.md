# Runway prompts: "Two universes, one applicant"

**What to do:** in Runway, choose image-to-video, upload the key frame (the two-universes picture) as the first frame, set 9:16 and 5-8 seconds, paste one prompt below, generate one clip per prompt. Stitch the clips in an editor and add the text and numbers there (AI video draws text badly). Generate the other shots as pictures first, then animate each as image-to-video; use the same style line in every picture prompt.

Video generation is not included in the free Runway plan; images are.

## Style line (add to every picture prompt)
```
Flat editorial motion-graphics style, deep navy background, warm amber and soft teal accents, green and red glows, soft studio lighting, subtle film grain, minimal, empty space at the top and bottom, blank cards with no writing.
```

## Shot 1 - the split (image-to-video from the key frame)
```
The screen splits slowly down the middle into two parallel universes. The same blank application card floats in both halves; the left card glows green, the right card glows red. The thin line behind each card shifts a little. Slow, steady camera push-in. Calm and clean.
```

## Shot 2 - the boundary and the dots (picture prompt, then animate)
Picture:
```
A field of small floating dots arranged along a horizontal score line, a thin vertical glowing line through the middle; dots to the right glow green, dots to the left glow red; one slightly larger dot just right of the line. [style line]
```
Motion:
```
The thin vertical line drifts slightly left, then right, then back. The one larger dot stays still; it turns red, then green, then red as the line crosses it. All other dots keep their colour. Slow, smooth.
```

## Shot 3 - a hundred universes (picture prompt, then animate)
Picture:
```
A 10 by 10 grid of small identical square windows, each showing a tiny dotted field with a thin vertical line at a slightly different position, one dot in each window glowing either green or red. [style line]
```
Motion:
```
The camera slowly pulls back over the grid. Windows flicker one by one: most show the dot green, a smaller share show it red. No new objects appear.
```

## Shot 4 - near the line, far from the line (picture prompt, then animate)
Picture:
```
A field of dots with a thin vertical line; a soft amber band around the line; dots far from the band are steady green or red, dots inside the band flicker in between. [style line]
```
Motion:
```
Dots inside the amber band flicker between green and red. Dots outside it stay perfectly still. Slow zoom into the band.
```

## Shot 5 - the universes agree (picture prompt, then animate)
Picture:
```
Many thin vertical lines slightly apart from each other over a field of dots, all glowing amber. [style line]
```
Motion:
```
The thin lines slide together into one single steady line. The flicker stops and the highlighted dot turns steady green. Calm resolve.
```

## Tips
* Describe motion in positive terms ("stays still", "slides together") instead of long lists of things to avoid.
* One idea per clip; keep camera moves slow.
* If a clip adds letters or faces, blur that area in the editor or reduce the number of objects in the picture prompt.
* The numbers (56 of 100, 55 of 300, 284 of 300) go in as captions from `STORYBOARD_UNIVERSES.md`.
